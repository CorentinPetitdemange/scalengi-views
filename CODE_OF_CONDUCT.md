# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Scalengi Views to be a welcoming, professional, and open project, regardless of contributors' experience, identity, or background.

## Expected behaviour

- communicate respectfully and precisely;
- welcome feedback and reasoned disagreement;
- favour decisions that benefit the project and its users;
- respect the confidentiality of information received;
- acknowledge contributions and sources.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, disclosure of private information, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Incidents may be reported privately to the maintainer at [corentin@scalengi.com](mailto:corentin@scalengi.com). Do not use a public issue for a confidential report.

Reports will be reviewed confidentially and proportionately. Behaviour that is incompatible with this code may result in content removal or temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.
